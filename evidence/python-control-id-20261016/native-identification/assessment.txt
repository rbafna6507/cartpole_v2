What the measurements can identify:
  Raw encoder count resolution, conditional angle/timing noise, commanded governor
  trajectory, control capture/hold performance and repeated pretrial stability are observed.
  Verified fixed-cart passive data can identify L_eff=I_pivot/(m_pole*COM_distance) and
  gamma (plus alternative effective friction laws if data distinguish them).
  Commanded-input forced data can identify effective composite gain/delay conditional
  on calibrated angle, the model and prediction gates. Independent cart measurement is
  not mathematically required for that restricted effective model, but is required for
  physical tracking/missed-step/slip or actuator-latency claims.
  True cart acceleration/lag require independent calibrated x(t) and clock mapping;
  step counts are not physical cart truth. Acceleration estimation needs noise-aware
  derivatives/weak displacement methods and its own uncertainty.
  Cart mass, pole mass, force-per-command, hinge torque damping and separate motor
  friction are not identified by angle plus commanded acceleration. Measure mass/COM
  independently; then L_eff can give inertia and gamma can give physical damping under
  a validated friction model. Motor force/friction identification also needs force/current
  calibration or independent load experiments. A successful model-free RL policy would
  not retroactively identify those physical quantities.
