REUSABLE SCORER (Python 3 + NumPy only)
python3 /tmp/cartpole-jitter-20261016/score_jitter.py /absolute/path/report.json.gz --json-output /tmp/cartpole-jitter-20261016/new-score.json

Multiple report paths, directories, and quoted glob patterns are supported.
Default common window: 15 seconds beginning 2 seconds after capture into the
longest continuous mode3 bout. Shorter/gappy windows are marked non-comparable.
--skip and --window can override this, but compare identical settings.

The JSON retains the full parameter subset, first/all capture states, initial
hanging offset, longest/total observed balance durations, balanced-at-deadline,
return success, faults/misses/I2C delta, comparison-window bounds, native gaps,
angle/cart extrema, spectral bands, four feedback terms, requested/applied jerk,
and acceleration tracking error. Capture inward_velocity is positive when moving
toward center, negative when moving toward the nearer rail. This is a useful
stratification variable; hanging offset alone previously failed to predict success.

Spectra: 250 Hz interpolation on board ticks, Welch periodic-Hann 2048 samples,
50% overlap, segment mean removed, trapezoidal band integration. NumPy Welch was
verified numerically against scipy.signal.welch. At most 20 ms native gaps are
allowed for a comparable window. Jerk is the native interval-average derivative;
a 4 ms interval at the 150 limit implies sustained limiting across that interval,
but the fraction is not an exact 1 kHz instantaneous duty. Longest-hold durations
are observed lower bounds. Full return requires report ok, REHOME then IDLE,
centered/settled commanded cart, and final fault0.

QUANTIZATION AND CANDIDATE REPLAY
bw10_noise_and_candidates.py and observer-replay-and-quantization.txt implement
the exact discrete 1 kHz observer transfer and replay the unchanged recorded
trajectories. This is not a closed-loop plant simulation or stability proof.
12-bit encoder quantum=.08789deg. Observed raw4-9 RMS=.79/1.40deg (~9/16 counts),
raw9-30=.94/1.00deg (~11 counts). Ideal uniform white one-count quantization at
1 kHz gives only .004/.008 m/s2 demand RMS in those bands after the bw10 observer;
actual demand is hundreds of times larger. Direct rounding alone cannot explain
the amplitude. Quantization could still seed a feedback oscillation. The records
cannot distinguish physical vibration, flexible mechanics, or magnetic disturbance.
In ag7 one raw angle jump is7.82deg in4ms at59.366s; no independent camera evidence
is available to classify it.

NARROW EXPERIMENTS
1) bw10 -> 9 only, preserve pw/pz/poles/jerk and all swing/capture parameters.
Recorded-trajectory replay predicts 4-9 demand RMS reduction3.9-4.7%, 9-30~9.5%,
with another5-6deg demand phase lag at6-10Hz. Repeat complete successful holds
before interpreting the change, because initial state/run variability is larger.
2) bw8 only if bw9 remains eligible: replay predicts4-9 reduction8.7-10.5%,

so capture must still be checked even when energy/capture parameters remain fixed.

also affects capture/rail prediction. pz.85->.8 reduces rate gain by only2.15%
while changing low-frequency feedback. Preserve bw10 as the reloadable baseline.
